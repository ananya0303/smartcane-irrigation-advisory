ABOUT THE SYSTEM

Intelligent Irrigation for Sugarcane

Combining artificial intelligence and sensor technology for smarter farming.

THE PROBLEM

Why Smart Irrigation?

Efficient irrigation is important for sugarcane cultivation. Farmers need timely information about field and environmental conditions to decide when irrigation is required.

SmartCane is designed as an irrigation advisory platform where sensor-based field information can be combined with AI-based analysis to support irrigation decisions.

🎯 Main Objective

To provide irrigation advisory for sugarcane crops using AI and sensor-based technology.
